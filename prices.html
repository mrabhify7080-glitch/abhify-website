Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, contracts, and discounts.

Are there any hidden fees or setup charges?
No hidden fees whatsoever. All setup fees are currently 100% waived during our launch offer window. The price you see is exactly what you pay for agency management. Paid advertising budget (Google or Meta) is billed directly by Google/Meta to your own card.
How does billing frequency work?
Our standard packages are billed monthly in advance via UPI, Net Banking, or Credit Card. If you choose quarterly billing, you receive an automatic 15% discount; annual commitments receive 25% off.
Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime?
Yes! We never lock you into rigid annual contracts. You can upgrade or adjust your scope at any time. If you wish to cancel, we simply require 15 days written notice before your next billing cycle.
