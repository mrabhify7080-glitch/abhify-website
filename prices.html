Common Billing Questions

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Direct, transparent answers regarding founding client offers, deliverables, and billing terms.

What is the Founding Client Launch Offer?
To build strong initial client partnerships and documented case studies, we are offering an introductory 50% discount on all three monthly packages. Starter Growth is ₹2,999/mo (standard ₹5,999), Business Growth is ₹5,999/mo (standard ₹11,999), and Premium Growth is ₹9,999/mo (standard ₹19,999).
Is paid advertising spend included in these package prices?
No. Your monthly package fee covers our agency management: strategy, creative post design, reel editing, audience targeting, ad copy, and performance reporting. Your ad budget is paid directly by you to Meta (Facebook/Instagram) or Google via your own card or billing account.
Do you provide camera crews or on-site filming?
Package reels are edited from raw smartphone video clips, property walkthrough footage, or photos that you share with us. We add dynamic hooks, cuts, background audio, and subtitles. Physical on-location filming and professional camera crew services are quoted separately based on project requirements.
What are the realistic performance review timelines?
Each package has a defined initial review milestone: 30 days for Starter Growth, 30 to 60 days for Business Growth, and 60 to 90 days for Premium Growth. Digital marketing algorithms and local search profiles require steady consistency; we do not make unrealistic promises of overnight leads or guaranteed rankings.
Are there long-term contracts or lock-ins?
All packages operate on a flexible month-to-month basis. We require 15 days written notice before your next billing date if you wish to pause or discontinue services.
