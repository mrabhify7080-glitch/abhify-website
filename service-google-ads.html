Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Google Ads

Everything you need to know about budgets, billing, and lead quality.

Is ad spend included in your management fee?
No, your ad budget is paid directly to Google via your own debit/credit card or Google billing profile. Our management fee covers strategy, keyword research, ad copywriting, conversion tracking setup, daily bid management, and continuous ROI optimization.
What is the minimum recommended ad budget?
For local service companies, real estate, and clinic practices in Uttar Pradesh and North India, we recommend an ad spend budget of ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 per month (approx. ₹500 to ₹1,000 per day) for statistically significant testing and consistent lead flow.
How do you prevent junk or competitor clicks?
We use strict match-type architecture (phrase and exact match), add 200+ negative keywords before launch, disable Google display network expansion on search campaigns, set precise geographic radius boundaries, and monitor search terms daily to block zero-intent queries immediately.
