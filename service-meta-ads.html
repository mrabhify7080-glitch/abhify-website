Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Meta Ads

Direct answers regarding lead quality, budgets, and ad design.

How do you prevent junk or invalid phone number leads?
Standard instant forms auto-fill outdated phone numbers from old user profiles. We eliminate this by configuring 'Higher Intent' forms that require manual input confirmation, adding qualifying multiple-choice questions, or routing prospects directly into WhatsApp where they message you from their active personal phone.
Do you design all the images and video creatives?
Yes! Our in-house design team produces 8 to 12 custom static graphics, carousel cards, and motion reels each month. If you have raw footage of your property, showroom, or clinic, we edit and polish it into stop-scrolling ad creatives with dynamic subtitles.
What daily budget is required to see results?
We recommend a starting ad budget of ₹500 to ₹1,000 per day (₹15,000 to ₹30,000/month) for local campaigns in Unnao, Kanpur, or Lucknow. This gives Meta's algorithm enough data points to find your ideal buyers and stabilize your cost-per-lead.
